AR Measure — Support

Point. Tap. Measure. AR Measure turns your iPhone or iPad into an intelligent tape measure, level and room scanner.

Frequently asked questions

  • How accurate is it? On devices with LiDAR (iPhone Pro and iPad Pro models), measurements typically land within a centimeter or two of reality. On other devices the App uses advanced camera tracking; good lighting and textured surfaces improve accuracy.
  • Why does the App say "Move iPhone slowly"? AR tracking needs visual detail. Move slowly and aim at surfaces with texture and good light.
  • Room Scan is not available on my device. Room scanning requires the LiDAR scanner (iPhone Pro / iPad Pro models). You can still measure rooms point by point in Area mode.
  • Where is Measure Assistant? The assistant requires Apple Intelligence on a supported device. Everything else works without it.
  • How do I export? Open a project and tap the share icon for a PDF report or CSV. Floor plans export as image or PDF from a room scan.
  • Is my data private? Yes. Everything stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
