Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the AR Measure app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. The App is designed to work entirely on your device and to collect nothing.

Information we do not collect

  • The App has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or any personal information.
  • We do not transmit any data to our servers — the App has no servers.

Camera, measurements and room scans

The App uses your device's camera, motion sensors and — on supported devices — the LiDAR scanner solely to measure objects and spaces in augmented reality. All measuring, room scanning and floor plan generation happen on your device. Camera frames are processed live and are never stored or uploaded. Your measurements, photos, projects and room scans are stored locally in the App's storage and never leave your device unless you explicitly export or share them using the iOS share sheet.

Apple Intelligence

On supported devices, the optional Measure Assistant feature uses Apple's on-device foundation models to answer questions about your stored measurements. Processing happens on your device through Apple's frameworks. The App does not send your measurements to any third-party AI service.

Microphone and voice notes

The microphone is used only if you choose to dictate a voice note. Speech is converted to text using Apple's speech recognition, on device where supported, and only the resulting text is saved to your project.

Sharing and exports

When you export a report (PDF, CSV), photo, floor plan or 3D model (USDZ) and share it, the file goes only to the destination you choose (AirDrop, Files, Mail, etc.). We have no access to it.

Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
