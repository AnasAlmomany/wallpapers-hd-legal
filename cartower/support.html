Car Tower — Support

Car Tower: Crane Stack is a one-tap stacking game: swing the crane, drop the car, and build the tallest tower of cars you can, from the street all the way to space.

How to play

  • Tap anywhere to release the car from the crane. Land it centered on the car below for a PERFECT drop.
  • Chain perfect drops for combos and bonus points. 8 in a row in SPACE RACE triggers OVERDRIVE.
  • Overhangs make the tower lean. Land your next cars on the high side to straighten it before it topples.
  • Wide vehicles like buses and trucks make a stable base; small cars are harder to land.

SPACE RACE

  • Stack in waves of 8 cars. Every 2 waves you reach a pit stop and pick 1 of 3 perks.
  • The higher you climb, the bigger your altitude multiplier: Skyline, Cloud Deck, Stratosphere, Orbit, Deep Space and the Mothership.
  • You start with 3 hard hats. A miss or a collapse costs one; when they're gone, the run ends.
  • The Daily Launch gives every player the same cars and twists each day.

Common questions

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game? Yes. Progress is stored only on your device.

Does it work offline? Yes, the whole game plays offline.

Can I turn off sound or vibration? Yes, from Settings in the pause menu or the main menu.
