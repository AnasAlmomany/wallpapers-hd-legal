Beneath — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Beneath ("the app") is designed so that your data stays on your device. We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data.

Location

Your location is used only on your device to calculate which cities lie in the direction you point your phone. It is never stored by us or transmitted to our servers — we do not operate any servers.

Motion & sensors

Compass and motion data are processed entirely on your device to detect where you are pointing.

Third-party services

To show city details, the app fetches public information directly from Wikipedia/Wikimedia Commons (photos and descriptions) and Open-Meteo (weather). These requests include approximate coordinates of the city you are viewing — not your identity. Map imagery is provided by Apple Maps under Apple's privacy terms.

On-device AI

City summaries generated by Apple Intelligence run entirely on your device.

Your discoveries

Your passport of discovered cities is stored only on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.
