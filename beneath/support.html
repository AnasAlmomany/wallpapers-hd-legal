Beneath — Support

Beneath shows you what cities lie on the other side of the Earth from wherever you are — just point your phone.

Tips

  • Sweep slowly at the horizon in Sky mode; tilt down to aim deeper through the planet.
  • If directions seem off, wave your phone in a figure-8 to calibrate the compass.
  • Use the pin button to freeze your aim so you can tap cities precisely.
  • The core experience works offline; photos, weather, and street previews need a connection.
