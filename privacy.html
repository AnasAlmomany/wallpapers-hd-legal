Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Wallpapers HD: 4K Themes app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. We built the App to work without accounts and to collect as little as possible. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Information we do not collect

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, or precise location, and we do not sell your personal information.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage & diagnostics (Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics, by Google): anonymous events such as screens viewed, features used, and crash reports, used to fix bugs and improve the App.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): we show ads to keep the App free. Ad partners may use a device advertising identifier to serve and measure ads. On iOS, personalized advertising is only used if you allow tracking when prompted (App Tracking Transparency); otherwise you still see non‑personalized ads.
  • Remote configuration (Firebase): anonymous, to enable or tune features remotely.

Photos

When you save a wallpaper, the App writes the image to your device's photo library using the permission you grant. The App does not read, upload, or scan your existing photos.

Purchases

The optional one‑time "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. Whether the App is ad‑free is stored only on your device and via your Apple ID's purchase history for restore.

Wallpaper content

Wallpapers are retrieved from third‑party image sources for display and download. Your searches are sent to these services to return results; they are not linked to your identity by us.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Manage ad tracking in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Remove ads entirely with the one‑time in‑app purchase.
  • Reset your advertising identifier in iOS Settings.

Third‑party services

These providers process data under their own policies: Google Firebase, Google AdMob, and Apple.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change accordingly. Continued use of the App means you accept the current version.
