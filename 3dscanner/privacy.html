Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the 3D Scanner app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. The App is designed to work entirely on your device and to collect nothing.

Information we do not collect

  • The App has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or any personal information.
  • We do not transmit any data to our servers — the App has no servers.

Camera and scans

The App uses your device's camera and LiDAR sensor solely to capture 3D scans of objects. All capture, processing, and 3D reconstruction happen on your device. Your scans are stored locally in the App's storage and never leave your device unless you explicitly export or share them using the iOS share sheet.

Sharing and exports

When you export a model (USDZ, GLB, glTF, OBJ, STL, 3MF, PLY, XYZ) and share it, the file goes only to the destination you choose (AirDrop, Files, Mail, etc.). We have no access to it.

Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
