3D Scanner — Support

Requirements

  • Scanning requires an iPhone Pro model with a LiDAR sensor (iPhone 12 Pro or newer Pro/Pro Max).
  • iOS 17 or later.
  • Viewing, exporting, and sharing existing scans work on any supported iPhone.

Scanning tips

  • Scan in a bright, evenly lit space; avoid reflective or transparent objects.
  • Place the object on a surface with some texture and walk slowly around it.
  • Keep the whole object inside the capture box and complete a full circle.
  • Reconstruction runs on your device and can take a few minutes — keep the app open.

Export formats

Every scan can be exported as USDZ, GLB, glTF, OBJ (zipped with materials), STL, 3MF, PLY, or XYZ — for AR, Blender, Unity, Unreal, three.js, 3D printing slicers, and point-cloud tools.
