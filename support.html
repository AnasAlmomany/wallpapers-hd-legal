Frequently asked

How do I remove ads?

Tap the gold "Remove Ads" button on the Home screen and complete the one‑time purchase. It's yours forever.

I bought Remove Ads on another device.

Open the Remove Ads screen and tap "Restore Purchase" — it re‑enables ad‑free using your Apple ID.

How do I set a wallpaper?

Tap a wallpaper to open it, download it to your Photos, then set it from the Photos app as your lock or home screen background.

Where do downloads go?

Saved wallpapers are added to your device's photo library.
